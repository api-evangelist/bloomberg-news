New conversational AI interface transforms how financial professionals discover, analyze, and act on market intelligence Bloomberg today introduced ASKB, a powerful new conversational AI interface, now in beta, that redefines how financial professionals interact with the Bloomberg Terminal®. Purpose-built for investment research, ASKB brings agentic AI directly into the Terminal – helping investors accelerate discovery, streamline analysis, and generate insights with greater speed and confidence.
